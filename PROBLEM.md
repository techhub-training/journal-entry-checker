# Problem statement

Fill this in first (recipe step 1), before any code. Five short answers:

**Who:** who will use the tool, and when?

**The problem:** what goes wrong for them today, without it?

**The tool:** what does it do, in one sentence?

**What "right" means:** how will you know the tool's answers are correct? (This becomes your tests.)

**Out of scope:** what the tool will not do.
